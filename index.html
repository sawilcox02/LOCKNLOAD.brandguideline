Brand
Guidelines

The identity, voice, visual system and experience rules for LOCKNLOAD, the premium modular toy blaster system. Use this as the reference for every product, space, campaign and AI-assisted application.

CHAPTER 01

Brand
Experience

Who LOCKNLOAD is, why it exists, who it serves, and how it sounds.

01.1

Products &
Services

Brand Name
LOCKNLOAD
Core Offerings

A premium modular toy blaster system featuring a universal base, interchangeable attachments, and collectible accessories.

The experience extends beyond the products through interactive retail environments, recreational arenas, and community events.

01.2

Brand
Substance

Brand Story

Play is more than the products we use. It’s the friendships we form, the challenges we overcome, and the experiences we share.

LOCKNLOAD was created to bring people together through a universal modular blaster system that celebrates individuality without sacrificing connection.

Inspired by the freedom of building and the excitement of recreational competition, LOCKNLOAD makes it easy to experiment, share, and discover new ways to play.

Whether you’re building your own loadout or joining your team for the first time, everyone starts on equal ground.

Purpose

To bring people together through shared experiences that inspire creativity, camaraderie, and the excitement of play.

Mission

To create accessible, customizable play experiences that encourage creativity, teamwork, and meaningful connections through friendly competition.

Vision

A world where play brings people together, individuality is celebrated, and everyone has the freedom to participate.

Values
V1

Camaraderie

Teamwork, connection, and shared experiences.

V2

Self‑Expression

Individuality through customization, experimentation, and personal playstyles.

V3

Sportsmanship

Fair competition, mutual respect, and positive interaction.

V4

Longevity

Durable products, lasting relationships, and a modular system that evolves with its players.

Guiding Principle

Every application reinforces the relationship between individuality and connection. Never portray competition as hostility, customization as exclusivity, or individuality as separation from the community. Personalization is never a requirement for belonging or an automatic competitive advantage.

01.3

Brand
Positioning

Audience

Preteens, teenagers, and young adults seeking creative, customizable, and socially engaging play experiences.

LOCKNLOAD appeals to casual players and dedicated enthusiasts alike, offering an approachable entry point with opportunities for deeper personalization, collecting, and recreational competition.

Participation can begin without product ownership: official rental kits and timed arena sessions welcome newcomers. Solo visitors, friend groups, private parties, and organized teams all have a way in.

Market & Competition
LEGOModular construction, creative freedom, premium quality, and collectible systems.
NerfRecreational toy blasters, competitive play, and enthusiast communities.
Laser Tag / Recreational ArenasOrganized competition, teamwork, and social experiences.
Unique Value Proposition

Unlike traditional toy blasters with fixed configurations, LOCKNLOAD offers a universal modular system that allows players to customize, exchange, and experiment with attachments using the same foundational platform.

By combining premium product design with accessible customization and community-driven experiences, LOCKNLOAD encourages individuality while bringing players together through shared play.

02.2

The
Emblem

Two mirrored L forms create an N in negative space, tying the name directly to the form.

The mirrored elements also read as two blasters, side by side, signalling connection, teamwork and shared play. Its interlocking geometry reflects modularity and adaptability while the balanced angular structure conveys stability and perseverance.

Together, the mark becomes a concise symbol of individual expression, collective strength, and limitless play.

02.6

Textures &
Patterns

LOCKNLOAD’s graphic language blends graffiti paint textures with technical blueprint patterns. The energetic streaks signal self-expression and energy, while the blueprints highlight invention and modular design. Each faction has its own color and treatment, which celebrates different playstyles but keeps everything part of one shared system.

Graffiti paint

Movement, energy, individuality and self-expression through expressive paint treatments and dynamic forms. Faction colors apply only to their own treatments.

Technical blueprint

Modularity, customization, engineering and structural precision through organized geometric detail. Never arbitrary technical markings or unrelated motifs.

02.7

Composition
& Restraint

Minimal, clean industrial composition is the default. Street-culture graphics are controlled accents, not an instruction to fill space. One clear focal point, open negative space, few graphic layers. Drag the slider to see where a layout stops working.

    Copy economy

    One short headline, one concise supporting line, one clear call to action, and only necessary logistics. Never invent schedules, locations, URLs or registration details. Use marked placeholders.

    Grid & Layout

    Intentional alignment, consistent spacing, and clear relationships. Modular grids echo the interchangeable product platform. Layouts may be asymmetrical and dynamic, but always sit on a deliberate structure.

    Scale

    Large environmental graphics prioritize immediate recognition; small applications prioritize legibility and essential information. If a format can’t hold an asset without compromising it, request an approved variation.

    Motion

    Responsive, energetic, purposeful. Transitions may reference mechanical assembly, modular connections, directional movement, or the rhythm of competitive play. Movement supports the message, never decoration alone.

    Accessibility

    Sufficient contrast, legible type, recognizable navigation and intuitive interaction. Visual expression never compromises function.

    02.8

    Imagery &
    Materials

    Photographic Realism

    Believable arena lighting, restrained saturation, natural contrast and physical materials. Avoid excessive neon, bloom, unrealistic luminous surfaces and theatrical sci-fi color casts. For arena imagery, favor wider environmental compositions with candid, naturally motion-blurred participants over posed close-ups, and keep the architecture readable.

    Materials
    MetalConcreteRubberSynthetic surfaces

    Industrial materials signal durability, functionality and engineered quality, never deterioration or neglect. Balance them with expressive graphics, vibrant color, and evidence of human activity.

    Product Fidelity

    Show products with realistic proportions and believable construction. Preserve the supplied blaster designs and attachment systems. Do not invent or alter configurations without authorization.

    03.1

    The
    Products

    Blaster System

    The modular blaster system, VOLT, is the base blaster for all players. It can be used by itself or with added attachments for personalized play styles.

    Faction Attachments

    Interlocking pieces themed to one of three factions (FOCUS, BREACH, or ADAPT) are specialized for different styles of play. Because the attachments are modular, players can tune their blasters to their needs: mixing and matching, trading, and testing endless combinations.

    03.2

    Flagship
    Store

    Spatial Experience

    Branded wall murals, concrete futuristic architecture, and walls of blaster arsenals ready for play.

    Within the building is an in-store arena where guests test, play, and challenge one another in a community setting. The arena is visible from anywhere inside the store, encouraging players to get in on the action.

    03.3

    Multi-
    Sensory

    Sound

    Footsteps on concreteRushing airFoam burstsMatch buzzersCalloutsCheers

    The rhythmic echo of quick footsteps against concrete, the rush of air as players round corners, and the sharp, satisfying bursts of foam blasters firing. Match buzzers cut through the arena as teammates exchange callouts, encouragement, and cheers. Together, these sounds capture the adrenaline of friendly competition and the energy of a team working as one.

    Smell

    Cool metalAerosol paintFresh rubberSynthetic foam

    The cool, metallic scent of industrial machinery blends with subtle traces of aerosol paint, fresh rubber, and synthetic foam. Clean yet rugged, these scents evoke a futuristic, urban environment where creativity meets engineered precision, balancing the raw energy of street culture with the innovation of modern play.
